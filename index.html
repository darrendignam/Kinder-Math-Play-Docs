Maths worksheets for little fingers

Kinder Math turns a tablet into a stack of friendly worksheets for children who are learning to count, add and take away.

{% include store-links.html %}

What it does

  • 🎈 Pictures to count

    Every number on a sheet is shown as balloons, animals, squares or your own photos, laid out in ten-frames like in the classroom.

  • 🔢 A big number pad

    Children tap an answer box and pick the number. No keyboard pops up, and nothing to type.

  • ✏️ Digital paper

    Write anywhere on the sheet with a finger or stylus, in six colours. Rub out, undo, and use the dot grid to work it out.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Grown-ups set the sheets

    Make worksheets in seconds, from easy adding up to numbers to 100. Check results with your child's own writing.

  • 🖨️ Print it too

    Print any sheet on A4, or save it as a PDF, for practice away from the screen.

  • 🔒 Private by design

    No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts. It works offline, and nothing your child does leaves the device. Privacy policy.
