# Privacy policy

_Last updated: {{ page.last_updated }}_

Kinder Math is a maths worksheet app for young children, made by {{ site.author }}. This
policy covers the Kinder Math apps for Android and iOS, and this website.

## The short version

Kinder Math does not collect, send or share any information. Everything stays on your
device.

## What the app stores, and where

To work, the app keeps these on your device, in the app's own private storage:

- the children's names and the animal each one picked
- worksheets, answers, results and anything written or drawn on a sheet
- your settings
- photos you add to make your own picture sets

None of this is sent anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The app
works with no internet connection.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or
Google backup) may include the app's storage, under that service's own privacy policy.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers
- No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party services
- No in-app purchases
- No chat or sharing between users
- The only links to websites are on the credits page, inside the grown-ups' area

## Camera and photos

The app only uses photos when a grown-up chooses one in the grown-ups' area to make a
picture set. The photo is cropped on the device and stored only in the app.

## Printing and PDFs

When you print a sheet or save it as a PDF, the page goes to the printer or app you
choose in your device's own print or share screen.

## Children

Kinder Math is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, it
does not need parental consent to collect data, but the grown-ups' area is kept behind
a simple question so children cannot change settings or delete sheets by accident.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics,
advertising or third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP
addresses for security; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Bug reports, ideas and discussions are kept on GitHub and are **public**. Posting there
needs a GitHub account and is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement. Please do not
post children's names, photos or other personal details there.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
