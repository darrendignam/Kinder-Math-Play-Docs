Issues and discussions are on GitHub and are **public**, and need a free GitHub account.
Please do not post children's names, photos or other personal details. Screenshots are
welcome, but crop out anything personal first.

## When reporting a problem

It helps to include:

- the device (for example "Lenovo Tab M11" or "iPad 10th generation") and its Android or
  iPadOS version
- the app version, from your device's app settings or the store page
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Common questions

**I can't get into the grown-ups' area.**
Tap <span class="ui">🔒 Grown-ups</span> and type the answer to the times-table question
on the number pad. A wrong answer shows a new question.

**Can two tablets share the same children and sheets?**
Not at the moment. Each tablet keeps its own data, and nothing is sent anywhere.

**How do I move to a new tablet?**
Your device's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include the app's data when you restore
onto a new device.

**The answer boxes don't turn green or red.**
Check **Settings → Corrections**, and that **Answer boxes** is set to **Number pad**.
Handwritten answers are not checked.

**My child can't make new sheets.**
Turn on **Children can roll their own worksheets** in Settings.
